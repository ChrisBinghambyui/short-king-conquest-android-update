Short King Conquest 0.9
Touch: one finger places or drags the selected tool. Tap Pan (bottom right) and one finger moves the camera instead. Two fingers always pan and pinch-zoom. Tap any tile to inspect it. Rotate and Undo appear when they apply. Portrait and landscape both work.

Two layers. Surface: enemies, trees, boulders, defenses, and the rooms you carve into the mountain. Underground: rock, ore, belts, machines. One building per layer per tile. The View button cycles split / surface / underground; X-ray shows a faint view of the other layer.
1. Everything you place is a blueprint that a dwarf with the Construction skill must build. Trade Wagon (Economy): first is free, near the Gate. Wagons collect cargo, haul it underground to the Gate and back, and refuse input while away. Storage (2x2, Economy) takes belt input instead and pools it until full; dwarves never fill it.
2. Dig works on whichever view you use. Underground digging opens tunnels. Surface digging inside the mountain carves rooms (start at the Gate and go north). The Lab needs a 4x3 excavated room. Beds, desks and lanterns go in rooms or on open ground; doors need a room. Each dwarf wants a bed at a comfort level that rises with their best skill (tap a bed to see what it lacks). New dwarves need a free bed. Menu > Dwarves shows skill levels and per-skill on/off switches, and hiring.
3. Ore nodes hold a limited stock that slowly refills. Drain one and it stays shut until it is full again. The bar under each node shows its stock.
4. Clear removes trees and boulders so towers can see. Towers are built from bars and need ammo. Belt bars and ore into a Storage; a dwarf with Craftsmanship on mans each tower during raids and restocks it from Storage. The Belt Loader Rig upgrade unlocks the Ammo Tap: put one on the underground layer directly beneath a tower and a belt feeds it with no dwarf, at 80% fire rate. Crossbows hit air and ground. Mortars splash ground targets over trees but cannot hit flyers.
5. Raid (top right): buy-in sets enemy count and the base payout; difficulty multiplies enemy strength and payout. A win earns a map expansion: Actions > Expand, then tap a glowing block.

Saving. The game autosaves every 15 seconds and when you leave the app. Menu > Save forces one; New game erases it. A raid in progress is saved too.

Keys (PC): Space pause, F speed, V view, X x-ray, H pan mode, R rotate, Z undo, Esc close menus, WASD or right-drag pan, wheel zoom, number keys for common tools.

Dwarves

Toggle a skill OFF and that dwarf never takes those jobs. Levels raise speed; a comfortable bed adds more.
